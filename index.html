OUR STORY

From Small Tea Growers
to a Remarkable Cup

AFT Techno Trade grew from deep roots in Assam’s small tea-growing community. Built by people who understood the work behind every leaf, the factory was created with a simple ambition — to transform carefully selected tea into something truly distinctive.

Working with a network of growers around Dibrugarh, AFT brings together the land, the leaf and the people behind it.
